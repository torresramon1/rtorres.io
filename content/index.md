# Ramon Torres

![Ramon Torres](images/profile.jpg)

PhD Student · Computer Science and Engineering, Baskin School of Engineering · UC Santa Cruz

I am a PhD student in Computer Science & Engineering at [UC Santa Cruz](https://www.ucsc.edu), working with [Ram Sundara Raman](https://ramakrishnansr.com/) . My research sits at the intersection of privacy-preserving systems, internet measurement, and censorship circumvention. I study how anonymity and privacy tools like Tor perform and scale. I also measure how content and services are blocked or restricted around the world. My work combines systems building and empirical measurement to better understand the tools that keep the internet open and private.

Research interests: Network Security, Privacy, Sustainability
